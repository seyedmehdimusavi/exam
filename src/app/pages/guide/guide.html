login

Signing in

  1. Tap your name on the welcome screen.
  2. Type your 6-digit PIN on the keypad (or keyboard). It signs in as soon as the last digit is entered.
  3. You stay signed in on that device. Use logout at the bottom of the menu to sign out, e.g. to let someone else use it.

contrastThe theme button (next to sign out) switches between Auto, Light and Dark. Each device remembers its choice.

install_mobileOn a phone, open the site and choose Add to Home Screen (Safari: Share menu). It then opens like an app.

category

Categories and subjects

Open

Every question and exam belongs to a category (English, Geography, Visual Art, Science, Mathematics…) and a subject inside it (e.g. Mathematics → Fractions).

  1. New category: click New category, type a name, pick an icon and colour, then Save.
  2. Add a subject: type it in the box at the bottom of a category card and press Enter.
  3. Rename a subject: click its chip. Delete: the small cancel on the chip.
  4. Edit or delete a category: the more_vert menu on its card.

infoYou don't have to create subjects first: typing a new subject while adding a question creates it automatically. A category or subject that still has questions can't be deleted.

Adding a question and its answer

Open
  1. Category: tap one of the coloured buttons.
  2. Subject: pick from the list or type a new one.
  3. Type: choose the question type (see the table below).
  4. Question: write the question, then fill in the answer part for that type.
  5. Optionally set difficulty, points (default 1) and tags (comma separated).
  6. Click Save & add another. The form clears but keeps the category, subject and type, so the next question is quick.

editRecent questions below the form lists what was added (switch Mine / Everyone). Use edit to change one or delete to remove it. A question used in an exam can't be deleted.

Bulk upload from Excel

Open
  1. On the Upload page click Download Excel template. It has a Questions sheet with examples and a Help sheet listing your categories.
  2. Delete the example rows and add one question per row.
  3. Save the file, then drag it onto the page (or click to choose it).
  4. Check the preview: check_circle ready, info note, error needs fixing with the reason underneath.
  5. Fix a row with edit (opens the question form) or drop it with close.
  6. Click Import N questions. Rows with problems stay on screen so you can fix them afterwards.

Building an exam

New exam
  1. On Exams, click New exam and give it a title.
  2. Pick a category, and optionally one subject (or All subjects).
  3. Tick Time limit and set minutes if you want a countdown.
  4. Choose questions from the Question bank: tick them one by one, use Select all shown after searching/filtering, or click casino Add N random questions (optionally only easy/medium/hard).
  5. Reorder with the arrows or shuffle shuffle them in In this exam.
  6. Click Save exam, or Save & start to take it straight away.

editEdit or delete an exam from the more_vert menu on its card. Deleting an exam also deletes its results.

play_arrow

Taking an exam

Open
  1. Click Start on an exam card, then Start exam.
  2. Answer one question at a time. Use Next / Previous, or the numbered dots to jump (filled dots are answered).
  3. On the last question click Finish. You'll be warned about unanswered questions.
  4. The results page shows the score, what was right or wrong, and the correct answers.

saveAnswers are saved on the device as you go. If the page closes, reopen the exam and choose Continue. With a time limit, answers are submitted automatically when time runs out.

rate_review

Marking essays and paper exams

Essays

  1. On Exams, open the To grade tab (the red number shows how many are waiting).
  2. Open a result, type the points and optional feedback for each essay.
  3. Click Save marks in the bar at the bottom. The score updates straight away.

thumb_upIf a short answer was right but spelled a way you didn't list, click Accept this answer on the results page to give full marks.

Exams done on paper

  1. On Exams, click Import paper results and choose the exam.
  2. Download sheet, then fill in one row per person: Member (name), Date and either Total score or points for each question (Q1, Q2…).
  3. Choose file, check the preview and click Import.
today

Today

Open

A picture of one day: questions created, exams taken, right answers, time on exams and points earned, plus right/wrong by category, a 7-day chart and a list of everything done.

  • Choose whose day to see with the name chips (Everyone or one person). The choice is remembered on the device.
  • Use chevron_left chevron_right to look at earlier days.
  • Hover (or tap) bars and columns to see exact numbers.
trending_up

Progress

Open
  • Filter by person, period (7 / 30 / 90 days or all time) and category.
  • Trend compares the last 5 results with the 5 before (needs 6 or more results).
  • Score over time: hover a point (or focus the chart and use the arrow keys) to see each exam.
  • Practise next lists the weakest subjects under 80% (each needs at least 3 marked answers). Build an exam from those subjects next.
  • Results still waiting for essay marks are left out of averages until they're marked.
assessment

Report

Open
  • The overview lists every exam with results, average, best and last taken. Click a row to open it.
  • For one exam: average, best, lowest and average time, all results (sort by date or score), the hardest questions and the score for each question.
  • Export to Excel saves a file with a summary, the results and the question analysis.
help

Questions and answers

Forgot a PIN?
A PIN is the Firebase password. Reset it in Firebase Console → Authentication → Users → the person → Reset password (or set a new 6-digit one).
Does it work offline?
The app opens offline after the first visit and shows what was already loaded. Changes made offline are saved and sent when the connection returns.
Can two people use it at the same time?
Yes. Everything updates live on every device.
Why can't I delete a question or category?
It's still used. Remove the question from its exams (or move/delete the questions in the category) first.
